My rules

I wrote them for my patients and keep them at every consultation.

  1. I never push

    I often say: "Your face, your decision." My job is to recommend honestly and professionally. The decision is always yours.

  2. I don't compete on low prices

    Because I value my experience, my team and quality. People don't go to a cosmetologist for a discount, but for results and trust.

  3. I don't poach other specialists' patients

    That's not my philosophy. Everyone chooses their doctor by feel, by their view of beauty and by human qualities.

  4. I don't comment on other doctors' advice

    In my consulting room I only talk about you and my work. Other people's decisions are not my territory.

  5. Only the best training

    I don't save on knowledge or haggle over it. I invest in quality, safety and new possibilities for my patients.

  6. Technology isn't for looking good on Instagram

    It's for safety, effectiveness and the best solutions for patients.

  7. I'm always honest

    I don't try to please you. It's fine if you don't like what I say: a cosmetologist doesn't just "do a treatment", she guides you towards sensible, realistic care.

Becoming a doctor again

I came to Spain as an endocrinologist who spoke no Spanish. To practise again, I had to start almost from zero.

Today I'm a doctor again. And I haven't stopped: I keep studying, and that's what I bring to every appointment.

Marina

  1. Kharkiv

    Endocrinologist

    I dreamed of working at the regional hospital in Kharkiv, and I did. But what always drew me was the skin.

  2. Valencia

    A new language

    Yesterday you were a doctor; today, in a new country, formally you're not. A tutor four times a week, almost without breaks.

  3. DELE

    Passed

    It's not an exam where memorising answers is enough: you have to understand, write and speak fluently. After months of preparation: "APTO".

  4. Licence

    My medical number in Spain

    Diplomas, translations, apostilles, months of waiting. Those numbers meant far more than a registration: I can work as a doctor again.

  5. Today

    A doctor in Spain, with her own clinic

    Kostanda Beauty opened in November 2024 at Guillem de Castro 8. I keep training at international congresses such as IMCAS in Paris.

Questions

I don't know what my skin needs. Where do I start?

With a diagnosis. We analyse your skin, explain what we see and suggest a plan of treatments and home care. If you need nothing, we'll say so.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what the diagnosis shows, which is why we don't publish a price list. Before any treatment you get the exact price. The online consultation is free.

Can I have a consultation if I'm not in Valencia?

Yes. Online, we review your current routine and recommend products. Afterwards you get 5% off professional skincare for 7 days.

I have fillers from another clinic. Can you assess them?

Yes. A facial ultrasound shows whether product remains, where and how deep. If it needs correcting, hyaluronidase is applied in a targeted way under ultrasound guidance, not blindly.

Does it hurt?

Before we start we explain what you'll feel and choose the most comfortable technique. As one patient put it: «Всё прошло совершенно безболезненно» (it was completely painless).

Which languages do you speak?

Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.

Do you treat men too?

Yes. More and more men come to look after their skin: RF microneedling, facials, device treatments, trichology and massage.

How do I book?

By WhatsApp, by phone or by direct message on Instagram. We'll reply with a day and time.
